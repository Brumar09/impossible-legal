# Privacy Policy — Impossible

29 April 2026
29 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use the mobile game **Impossible** ("the App") on iOS and Android.

We designed Impossible to collect as little data as possible. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the current version.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
When you create a player profile inside the App, you give us:
- **Player name** (any text you choose — can be an alias)
- **Country** (selected from a list by you)

These are used exclusively to display your entries on the public leaderboard.

### 1.2 Gameplay data you submit
When your run ends, the App submits to our servers:
- Highest level reached in the run
- Total deaths
- Total run time
- Whether the run was completed (all levels cleared)
- A server-generated timestamp
- A randomly generated run identifier (UUID)

### 1.3 Information we do NOT collect
- We do **not** ask for your real name.
- We do **not** collect your email address, phone number, or postal address.
- We do **not** collect precise location (we never ask for GPS). The country shown is the one you select manually.
- We do **not** collect your contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
- We do **not** use cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.

### 1.4 Technical data
Our backend receives your public IP address when you contact our API (as with any internet service). We do not store the IP address with your player record.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information listed above only to:
- Operate the country and global leaderboards
- Maintain and improve the App
- Respond to support requests (if you contact us)

We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

---

## 3. Local Storage on Your Device

Your name, selected country, local best-run history, and total death counter are also stored **only on your device** using the operating system's secure local storage (AsyncStorage). Uninstalling the App removes all this local data.

---

## 4. Sharing Your Information

Your **player name, country, level reached, deaths, and run time** appear publicly on the in-app leaderboards. Anyone using the App can see them.

We do not share your data with any other third party, with the following limited exceptions:
- **Hosting / database providers** that store leaderboard entries on our behalf, under confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal requirements** — if compelled by valid legal process.
- **Business transfer** — if the App is ever sold, ownership of the data would transfer to the acquirer under this same Policy.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Impossible is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Leaderboard entries are retained indefinitely so that the all-time rankings remain meaningful. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (HTTPS in transit, access-controlled database) to protect the limited data we store. No internet service is 100% secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum necessary.

---

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (EU/EEA, UK, California, Brazil, etc.), you may have the right to:
- Access the data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your leaderboard entries
- Object to processing
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at velaroshopp@gmail.com with the player name and approximate run date. We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 9. International Transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country of residence. By using the App you consent to your minimal profile and run data being processed there.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services

The current version of Impossible does **not** integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. If a future version does, this Policy will be updated and the change announced in the App.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced inside the App.
